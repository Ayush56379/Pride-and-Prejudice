Literary Background

About Pride and Prejudice

Published in 1813, Jane Austen's Pride and Prejudice remains one of the most widely celebrated masterpieces in the English language. Set across the picturesque countryside of Hertfordshire and Derbyshire, the story chronicles the turbulent relationship between Elizabeth Bennet, the spirited daughter of a country gentleman, and Fitzwilliam Darcy, a rich and aristocratic landowner.

“It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune must be in want of a wife.”

This custom digital edition is presented as a high-fidelity digital PDF, combining nineteenth-century literary charm with modern digital readability. Every chapter, dialogue, and classical engraving has been meticulously preserved.
